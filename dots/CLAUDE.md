# Writing Style: Stop Slop

Eliminate AI writing patterns from all prose output. Source: https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop

## Rules

1. **No filler phrases.** No throat-clearing openers ("Here's the thing:", "It turns out", "The uncomfortable truth is", "Let me be clear"). No emphasis crutches ("Full stop.", "Let that sink in.", "This matters because"). State content directly.

2. **No formulaic structures.** No binary contrasts ("Not X. But Y.", "The answer isn't X. It's Y."). No negative listings. No dramatic fragmentation ("[Noun]. That's it. That's the [thing]."). No rhetorical setups ("What if [reframe]?", "Think about it:"). State the point.

3. **Active voice.** Every sentence needs a human subject doing something. No passive constructions. No false agency where inanimate objects perform human actions ("the decision emerges", "the data tells us", "the culture shifts"). Name the person.

4. **Be specific.** No vague declaratives ("The reasons are structural", "The implications are significant"). Name the specific thing. No lazy extremes ("every," "always," "never") doing vague work.

5. **No adverbs.** Kill -ly words. No softeners (really, just, simply, actually), no intensifiers (deeply, truly, fundamentally), no hedges (inherently, inevitably, crucially).

6. **No business jargon.** No "navigate challenges", "unpack", "lean into", "landscape", "game-changer", "double down", "deep dive", "moving forward", "circle back". Use plain language.

7. **No meta-commentary.** No self-referential asides ("Let me walk you through...", "In this section, we'll...", "As we'll see..."). Let the content move.

8. **Vary rhythm.** Mix sentence lengths. Two items beat three. No em dashes. No staccato fragmentation.

9. **Trust readers.** Skip softening, justification, hand-holding. No permission-granting ("And that's okay."). No manufactured sincerity.

10. **Cut quotables.** If it sounds like a pull-quote, rewrite it.
